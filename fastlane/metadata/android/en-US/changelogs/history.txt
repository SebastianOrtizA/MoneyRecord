## 1.4.2
- Every currency is now available: the list has all 159 ISO currencies, sorted by code, instead of 25.
- Picking a currency opens a list with a search box: type a code, a name or a symbol.

## 1.4.1
- Category trend now picks the category from a list that shows each one's total, instead of a wall of chips when you have many categories.

## 1.4.0
- New reports hub (More → More reports): Category trend, Payee report, Tags, Account report, Compare periods and Year in review.
- Category trend shows how spending in the categories you pick moves month by month.
- Payee report ranks where your money goes by payee; tap a row to see its transactions.
- Tags has its own page: see what each tag adds up to, rename, merge or delete tags.
- Account report shows income, expenses and transfers per account.
- Compare periods puts two periods side by side, category by category, with the change.
- Year in review summarises your year and can be shared as text or PDF.
- The Transactions filter now takes several categories at once.
- Saved filters: save the filters you use often under a name and apply them with one tap from the Transactions page.

## 1.3.1
- Fixed: the dates under the Forecast chart no longer overlap in Spanish and Portuguese.

## 1.3.0
- New Forecast page (More → Planning → Forecast): see how your balances may move over the next 30, 60 or 90 days from your recurring entries, scheduled entries and debt payments. These are estimates, not promises.
- Home has a Forecast card with the amount that is safe to spend until your next income.
- Low-balance warning: choose in Settings to be told 7, 14 or 30 days ahead when an account is expected to go negative.
- Budgets show how much you can still spend per day, and can carry over what you did not spend into the next period.
- New "All spending" budget: one limit for everything you spend, next to your category budgets.
- Suggest budgets: get a monthly limit for each category without a budget, based on your last 3 months, and tick the ones to create.
- Fixed: a launcher shortcut opened while the app lock is on now waits for you to unlock.

## 1.2.2
- The app's code is trimmed further, so it takes a little less space and memory.

## 1.2.1
- The app's code is now optimized as well as shrunk, so it is a little smaller and lighter on memory.

## 1.2.0
- Quick entries: save a repeat expense or income, like your morning coffee, in one tap from a chip on Home or on the add form. Undo is always offered, and More → Quick entries lets you edit and reorder them.
- A quick entry can leave the amount empty to ask each time, and the app suggests one after you repeat the same entry a few times.
- Long-press the app icon to add an Expense, Income or Transfer straight away, plus your top quick entries.
- Long-press the add button to start a new expense directly.
- Duplicate an entry from its details or with a swipe to add it again dated today.
- Choosing a payee now also fills in the account and the amount of the last entry.

## 1.1.1
- Transactions: group by day, category or account, and sort order, now live in the filter sheet (the ⋮ menu is gone).
- Groups start collapsed so the list is easier to scan; they open while you search or filter, and Expand all opens them at once.
- A long custom date range now fits on screen instead of being cut off.

## 1.1.0
- Adding an entry is faster: switch between Expense, Income and Transfer at the top, pick the account and Today or Yesterday with one tap, and Save stays above the keyboard and turns on once the amount is valid.
- Transactions are grouped by day with each day's total. Search is an icon in the toolbar, and every active filter shows as a chip you can remove.
- A period stepper (‹ October 2026 ›) replaces the period dropdown on Home, Transactions, Budgets and Reports.
- Reports: clearer tabs and chart colours, and you can press and hold a donut slice to see its amount.
- Budget cards show what is left or over with a progress bar, the most urgent first.
- More and Settings are grouped under headings, and Settings has a search box.

## 1.0.0
- Deleting a transaction, split or transfer no longer asks you to confirm: tap Undo in the message that appears to bring it back.
- New: a Get started card on Home walks you through the first steps (add an expense, back up your data, turn on reminders), and empty screens now tell you what to do next.
- Categories and Accounts have a search box, and Transfers, Cash flow, Net worth and Payees explain themselves when they are empty.
- Large text: amounts are never cut off, and categories, the balance caption and buttons fit at the biggest font sizes.
- Better with TalkBack: more controls have labels, tap targets are at least 48 dp and faint text is darker.
- Income is blue and expenses red everywhere, the Home tiles are calmer, and the status bar icons are readable on the purple bar.

## 0.19.2
- Savings challenges: a linear challenge can now start from a first day and grow by an amount per day. Type the increase and the app works out how many days you need. Doubling is no longer offered for new challenges; the ones you already have keep working.
- The challenge page has new filters: From start, This month (browse it month by month, as a calendar), Next 30 days and Date range, plus Only pending.
- Dates in the challenge grid no longer get cut off in some languages.
- Adding an entry: opening More details now scrolls down to the new fields.
- Icons: many more to choose from for expense and income categories, and a few more for accounts.

## 0.19.1

## 0.19.0
- MoneyRecord now speaks Portuguese (Brazil), French and Italian, on top of English and Spanish. It follows your phone's language, or pick one in Settings → Language.
- Receipt scanning understands French, Italian and Portuguese receipts: totals, subtotals, tax, change and dates.

- The challenge page opens on the next 30 days, with filters for this, last and next month, the goal's last 30 days and a date range, so long challenges stay light.

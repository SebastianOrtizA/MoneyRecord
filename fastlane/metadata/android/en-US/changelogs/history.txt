## 0.18.0
- Savings challenges: a savings game with daily amounts that double in levels or grow linearly, with optional rounding.
- Pick a destination account or pocket and each day moves the money there from the account or pocket you choose.
- The What's new pop-up lists each change on its own line and every version you missed.
- Backups now use format v7.
- Optimized app build.

## 0.16.0
- Pockets: set money aside inside an account and pick which pocket to take from when an expense dips into it.
- Variable-amount recurring bills you confirm each time.
- Customize Home (order, show/hide cards) plus an optional Debts card.
- A What's new pop-up after updates.
- Recent amounts now hide with Hide balances.
- Backups now use format v6.

## 0.13.1
- Amounts now show thousands separators as you type, so a long number is easy to read while you enter it.
- Also in the Transactions amount filter and trip category limits.

## 0.13.0
- Parties: split trips and shared expenses with friends, see who owes whom, settle up and share the summary, and record your own share as an expense.
- Backups now use format v5.

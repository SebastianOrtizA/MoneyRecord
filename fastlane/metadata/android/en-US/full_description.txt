MoneyRecord is a simple, private money tracker. Record what you spend and earn, see where your money goes, stay on budget and save for your goals. Your data stays on your phone: no sign-up, no ads.

<b>Fast entry</b>
• Add an expense, income or transfer in a few taps: amount first, then a category from a grid that puts your recent ones first.
• Scan a receipt: its total and date are read on your phone.
• Payees with suggestions, tags, receipt photos and descriptions, if you want them.
• Split one receipt across several categories.

<b>Accounts and currencies</b>
• Cash, bank, savings, credit cards… each with its own currency.
• Totals per currency, or converted into your main currency with daily exchange rates (optional).
• Hide the cents of the currencies that don't need them.

<b>Budgets and savings goals</b>
• Spending limits per category, per day, month or year: on track, near limit or over budget at a glance.
• Savings goals with a target date: how much to save each month, and whether your pace gets you there.

<b>Recurring and upcoming</b>
• Salary, rent, subscriptions: daily, weekly, every two weeks, monthly or yearly, starting today or on a future date.
• See what is coming in the next 30 days, with reminders before bills are due and when a budget runs out.

<b>Money lent and borrowed</b>
• Keep track of what friends owe you and what you owe them, with payments on a schedule.

<b>Reports</b>
• Spending and income by category, trends, cash flow and net worth.
• Tap a chart to see the transactions behind it.
• Export a PDF report or a CSV file of any period.

<b>Your data, your control</b>
• Works offline. No account, no ads.
• Full backups, by hand or automatic (daily, weekly or monthly) into any folder, Google Drive included, with an optional password.
• Import bank statements from CSV, with duplicates found and categories suggested.
• App lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock, and an encrypted database.
• Optional crash reports, off unless you turn them on.

<b>Made for you</b>
• Home-screen widget with your balance and quick add buttons.
• English and Spanish, light and dark theme, a quick setup on first start.

MoneyRecord is free and open source (AGPL-3.0): https://github.com/SebastianOrtizA/MoneyRecord

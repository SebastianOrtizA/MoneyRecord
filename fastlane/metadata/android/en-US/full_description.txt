MoneyRecord is a simple, private money tracker. Record what you spend and earn, see where your money goes, and stay on budget. Your data stays on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking.

<b>Fast entry</b>
• Add an expense, income or transfer in a few taps: amount first, then a category from a grid that puts your recent ones first.
• Optional description, tags, receipt photo (camera or gallery) and recurrence.

<b>Accounts and currencies</b>
• Cash, bank, savings, credit cards… each with its own currency.
• Balances in different currencies are shown side by side, never added together.

<b>Budgets</b>
• Spending limits per category, per day, month or year.
• Clear status at a glance: on track, near limit, over budget.

<b>Reports</b>
• Spending and income by category, with a donut chart.
• Trends, cash flow and net worth over the last months, budget vs. actual.
• Tap a chart to see the transactions behind it.

<b>Recurring transactions</b>
• Salary, rent, subscriptions: daily, weekly, every two weeks, monthly or yearly, with an optional end date. Missed occurrences are added when you open the app.

<b>Your data, your control</b>
• Full backup as a .zip file (with receipt photos), saved on the device or shared; restore with undo.
• Export transactions as CSV.
• Works offline. Optional crash reports, off unless you turn them on.

<b>Made for you</b>
• English and Spanish, light and dark theme, a quick setup on first start.

MoneyRecord is free and open source (AGPL-3.0): https://github.com/SebastianOrtizA/MoneyRecord
